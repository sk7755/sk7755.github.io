02 / Selected work

Research threads

Three geometric structures recur across the papers.

Thread 01

Width-constrained polygon partitioning

Minimum partitions under prescribed width and cut directions, followed by input-sensitive algorithms and matching lower bounds for orthogonal strip partitions.

  • parallel-cut structure for convex polygons
  • lattice-theoretic dynamic programming
  • convex, simple, and self-overlapping polygons
SWAT 2026 ISAAC 2025

Thread 02

Convex polygons and histogons

Algorithms for approximating a convex polygon by a connected union of equal-width rectangles, under inscribed and circumscribed containment.

  • axis-aligned and arbitrary orientations
  • unit, k-histogon, and free-width variants
  • contact-event characterization
Computational Geometry 2026 FSTTCS 2022

Thread 03

Largest inscribed unit rectangles

Computing a maximum-area rectangle with one prescribed side length inside a convex polygon, using geometric contacts and orientation events.

  • variable height and orientation
  • boundary-contact configurations
  • exact geometric optimization
Computational Geometry 2025
