Research

  • Computational geometry. Partitioning and covering polygons; containment and approximation of shapes; geometric lower bounds.
  • Algorithmic primitives for AI. Nearest-neighbour search, weighted selection in sorted lists, geometric optimal transport.
  • Learning-augmented algorithms. Online scheduling with predictions; neural combinatorial optimisation.

Currently working on dynamic k-nearest-neighbour search in high-dimensional non-Euclidean metrics.
